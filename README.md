# PassAbi AI 📚🤖

> **Learn. Understand. Pass.**

PassAbi AI is an AI-powered learning platform built to help students understand difficult topics, practice effectively, and prepare for examinations with personalized AI assistance.

Founded by **Uzezi Great Efezino**, PassAbi AI is designed with students in mind, with a focus on making AI-powered education simple, accessible, and practical.

## 🌐 Live Application

https://passabiai.vercel.app

## ✨ Features

### 🤖 AI Tutor
Ask questions and receive clear, step-by-step explanations designed to make difficult concepts easier to understand.

### 📚 Study Tools
PassAbi AI helps students:

- Understand difficult concepts
- Get simple explanations
- Generate examples
- Create quizzes
- Practice questions
- Review topics
- Prepare for examinations

### 📝 Exam Preparation
PassAbi AI is being developed to support students preparing for major Nigerian examinations, including:

- WAEC
- NECO
- JAMB
- School examinations

### 💬 AI Study Chat
Students can interact with the AI through conversations, ask follow-up questions, and continue learning from previous discussions.

### 👤 Student Accounts
Users can create accounts and access personalized features and study sessions.

### 📱 Responsive Design
PassAbi AI is designed to work across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop devices.

## 🎯 Mission

PassAbi AI was created around a simple idea:

> **Students should not only get answers — they should understand them.**

Our goal is to help students use artificial intelligence as a genuine learning tool rather than simply as a way to obtain answers.

PassAbi AI aims to make learning more accessible by combining AI with practical study tools that help students:

**Learn → Practice → Understand → Improve**
